# CreatorsHub Business Decision Framework

## Purpose

CreatorsHub exists to help creators make better business decisions.

The platform does not prioritize interesting observations.

It prioritizes useful decisions.

---

# Decision Hierarchy

Every recommendation should be evaluated in this order.

## 1. Protect the Business

Prevent losses.

Reduce unnecessary risk.

Maintain creator stability.

Examples:

- Revenue declining
- Sponsor at risk
- Upload consistency collapsing
- Audience retention falling rapidly

---

## 2. Grow the Business

Increase long-term value.

Examples:

- Audience growth
- Revenue growth
- Product sales
- Subscriber growth
- Customer retention

---

## 3. Improve Efficiency

Help creators accomplish more work with less effort.

Examples:

- Better upload scheduling
- Better content cadence
- Automation
- Cross-platform publishing

---

## 4. Experiment

Only after the business is healthy.

Examples:

- New content formats
- New revenue streams
- Platform expansion
- Marketing experiments

---

# Decision Rules

CreatorsHub should always prefer:

High impact

↓

High confidence

↓

Low effort

↓

Fast feedback

---

# Recommendation Rules

Every recommendation must:

Be actionable.

Be evidence based.

Have a clear reason.

Have an expected outcome.

Have a suggested timeframe.

---

# Recommendation Limit

CreatorsHub should normally recommend ONE highest-priority action.

Additional recommendations may exist internally.

Only the highest-value recommendation should be surfaced unless the creator explicitly requests more.

---

# Never Recommend

Actions unsupported by evidence.

Advice that contradicts available business data.

Generic motivational advice.

Recommendations that cannot reasonably be acted upon.

---

# Guiding Principle

CreatorsHub is not trying to answer every possible question.

CreatorsHub is trying to answer the next most important business question.
